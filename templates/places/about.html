How to use NearbyGo

NearbyGo lists places to visit within {{ radius_km|floatformat:0 }} km of Panadura and helps you put a few of them in order for a day out. You don't need an account.

  1. Browse places

    The Places page shows every listed place with its category and approximate distance from Panadura. Choose a category such as Nature or Religious to see only those places. Choose All places or Clear filter to see everything again.

  2. Read the details

    Each place has a description, opening hours, entrance fee, recommended visit time, facilities, transport and travel tips. When something hasn't been confirmed, the page says Not available or Not verified instead of guessing.

  3. Use the map

    The Map shows every place as a coloured marker. Select a marker to see its name and a link to its details. If the map can't load, the list under it still works.

  4. Build your plan

    Choose Add to plan on any place. On My plan you can move places earlier or later, add an optional arrival time, remove a place or clear the whole plan. Your plan is kept in this browser for about a week, and no personal details are collected.

Good to know

  • Distances are straight-line estimates from a point in Panadura. Road distances are usually longer.
  • Opening hours, fees and facilities can change. Confirm with the place before you travel, especially on public holidays and Poya days.
  • NearbyGo doesn't sell tickets, take bookings or payments, plan routes, or show live traffic, weather or bus times.
  • Only visit areas that are open to the public, and follow local signs and lifeguard advice at beaches.

For administrators

Sign in at the administration area to add or update categories and places. Each place needs a unique name, a category, a description, coordinates and a distance of {{ radius_km|floatformat:0 }} km or less to be active. Untick active to hide a place straight away if its information becomes unsafe or out of date. Set practical info verified on after checking hours and fees with the venue.
